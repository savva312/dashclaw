# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must make participation welcoming and
harassment-free. Be respectful, give actionable technical feedback, accept
corrections, and focus disagreements on ideas and evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
intimidation, sexualized conduct, publishing another person's private
information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
options on their GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this standard. Depending on severity and history,
responses may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal
from project spaces.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents
Dashclaw.
